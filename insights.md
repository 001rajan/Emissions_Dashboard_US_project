## Business Problem

Analyze greenhouse gas emissions across U.S. counties and identify patterns related to population, geography, and regional contribution.

## Solution

Built an interactive dashboard in Databricks SQL featuring:
- KPI Cards
- Geospatial Mapping
- Scatter Plot Analysis
- State-wise Emission Distribution
- Top Emitting County Rankings
- Interactive Filtering

## Outcome

The dashboard enables users to identify high-emission regions, compare county performance, and analyze environmental impact through data-driven visualizations.
